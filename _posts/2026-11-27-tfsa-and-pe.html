--- title: "TFSA and PE: Six Letters That Set Up Every Shot" category: "Training" author: "Forward Pickleball" redirect_from: - /blog/2026/11/27/tfsa-and-pe/ ---

Six body parts decide how well you hit the ball. Two short groups hold them: TFSA, then PE. TFSA is the chain, in the order it happens. PE is the hitting end.

TFSA is already a familiar set of letters in Canada, which makes the first half easy to remember.

TFSA

T — Toes

Stay on your toes, ready.

F — Feet

Feet move first, toward the ball.

S — Shoulder turns

Second, after the feet. The shoulder turn loads the shot.

A — Arm

Last, loose and relaxed, not locked straight.

PE

P — Paddle up

Paddle in front and ready.

E — Elbow soft

Soft bend, about 100°–120°, the shock absorber.

TFSA sets the shot up. PE finishes it. Run through the six letters before you hit, and the body does the work in the right order: toes, feet, shoulder turn, arm — then paddle and elbow.
