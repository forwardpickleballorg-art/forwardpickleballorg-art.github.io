Five paddles getting the most attention from players in November 2026. They are all power-first paddles, and they run from $179 to $350 before discounts — so the real question is not which one hits hardest, but which one fits your game and your budget. Prices below are list prices in US dollars, checked November 24, 2026, with the discount applied where it lowers the price.

This ranking is based on real data — community votes from thousands of players, not one person's opinion — and the list changes month to month.

1. Honolulu J6CR M105

Most powerful in Honolulu's lineup. Elongated 16.48″ x 7.52″, 16mm, Crystal Blue Endurance Surface, MPP Core Reactor with Dynamic PowerFlex and Control Joint.

Buy it if you want the top-ranked paddle of the month and a surface that holds its spin. Pass if your budget stops under $180 — even with the discount it is not the cheapest here.

2. Selkirk Boomstik ST

Follow-up to the original Boomstik ($333), launched Nov 10. BoomCore foam, Multistrata carbon, InfiniGrit Ultra, Sharktooth groove, adjustable MOI weights. Elongated and widebody, same price.

Buy it if you want Selkirk's newest power paddle and the MOI weights to tune the feel. Pass if $350 is more than you want to spend on a paddle — the $40 e-gift card does not bring the price down.

3. Bread & Butter Loco 2

Gen 4 foam-core power paddle. Space Dust Grit built to hold spin longer. Elongated and hybrid, both $220.

Buy it if you want a Gen 4 power paddle with a grit surface that keeps its bite. Pass if you want the lowest price on this list — the Luzz does the same job for less.

4. 11SIX24 Ultré Power 2

Most offensive shape in the Power 2 line. Gen 4 foam core, HexGrit (Tier 1 grit durability), highest firepower and pop in the line. UPA-A only, not USAP-approved.

Buy it if you want the most firepower and pop in the Power 2 line and a grit surface that holds its spin. Pass if you play USAP-sanctioned events, because it is UPA-A only — and check the Black Friday price first, because at $129.99 until December 4 it is a very different value than at $209.99.

5. Luzz Cannon XPP M1

Cheapest list price here. Gen 4 elongated power paddle, XPOP foam core, Green SiC Grit, swing weight around 122 (heavy, lots of plow). Dual USAP + UPA-A certified. Free paddle cover + edge tape with the Black Friday offer ending Dec 1, 2026 (PST).

Buy it if you want the lowest price on this list and a paddle that is legal in both USAP and UPA-A events. Pass if a heavy swing weight bothers you in fast hands battles.
