We believe pickleball is about more than just winning. It’s about having fun, improving our game, building friendships, and creating a welcoming community for everyone.

As our league continues to grow, we’d like to remind everyone of a few important expectations when stepping onto the court.

🏓 Play Fair & Include Everyone

Every player deserves to feel included and respected, regardless of skill level. Please avoid deliberately icing out or excluding a player by consistently directing shots away from them. While strategy is part of the game, our recreational leagues should encourage participation, fair competition, and opportunities for everyone to play.

🤝 Respect On-Court Calls

Players are responsible for making calls on their own side of the court. Respect those calls, even if you disagree. Honest mistakes happen, and no point is worth creating tension or conflict.

🔄 When in Doubt, Replay the Point

If there’s uncertainty or disagreement about a call, communicate respectfully. When appropriate and agreed upon by the players, simply replay the point with a re-serve. Keep the game moving and the atmosphere positive.

💬 Communicate with Respect

Competitive spirit is welcome, but arguing, intimidating others, making negative comments, or displaying poor sportsmanship is not. Encourage your teammates and opponents, and remember that everyone is there to enjoy the game.

❤️ Competition Is Great. Community Comes First.

We love seeing passionate, competitive matches, but let’s never lose sight of what makes our community special: our people.

Thank you for doing your part to keep our league a fun, fair, respectful, and welcoming place to play!

See you on the courts!
