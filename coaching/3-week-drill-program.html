Forward Pickleball · Coaching Program

3-Week Drill Program

A three-week coaching program — deliberate drills, zero wasted rallies. One focus per week, built from the drill library, and each week builds on the last.

The program

3 weeks · 3 drills
  • Week 1

    Third Shot Drop & Resets

    Topspin forehand third shot drops and transition-zone resets — the two shots that unlock the 4.0 game, with a relaxed 3–4/10 grip. Get to the kitchen on your terms instead of getting stuck in no-man's land.

    Open drill
  • Week 2

    Master the Return

    Hit deeper, more effective returns — push the ball into the deep zone to neutralize the serving team and take control of the point. A good return buys you the third shot; a short one gives it away.

    Open drill
  • Week 3

    Fast Hands Volley Drill

    Win the kitchen firefight — compact, calm volleys with no backswing. Includes the live firefight drill with progressions, plus counter-attacking the speed-up.

    Open drill
