7 Positioning Upgrades That Win You More Points

Where you stand wins more points than how you swing.

By Forward Pickleball

You don't need a new paddle or a harder shot. At the 3.0 to 4.0 level, where you stand wins more points than how you swing. Here are seven positioning upgrades to put into your game.

1. Serve and stay

Hold your ground after your serve until you see the return. The return is coming back deep — creeping in early leaves you scrambling backward to short-hop it. Stay back, read the return, then move in on your third shot.

2. Move up together on thirds

When your partner hits the third shot, move forward with them instead of staying planted at the baseline. Arrive at the kitchen as a pair, with no gap between you.

3. Give yourself room on the return

Start a step or two behind the baseline so a deep serve can't jam you. Give yourself room, then move in after contact.

4. Move diagonally through transition

Instead of running straight ahead in your own lane, move diagonally toward the kitchen, following the ball. Get stable before you hit. Move, stop, hit — then move again.

5. Hold the kitchen line

Toes near the line, paddle up, and take balls out of the air when you can. Hanging back gives up the court and lets balls drop at your feet. Points are won at the line — get there and hold it.

6. Recover to the middle after going wide

After chasing a wide ball, get back toward the middle of your side before the next shot comes, so you're ready for the reply.

7. Cover the middle together

The middle isn't a line down the court for each player to defend alone. Move with your partner, shift with the ball, and cover the middle together — talk to each other, and let the forehand in the middle take it when it makes sense.

Put it into your game

Pick one or two that felt familiar and work on them in your next games. Positioning is the fastest upgrade in pickleball — no new strokes required.
