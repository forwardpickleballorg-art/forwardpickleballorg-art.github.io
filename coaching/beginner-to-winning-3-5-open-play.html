🏓 From Beginner to Winning in 3.5 Open Play — In Just 5 to 7 Hours of Training! 🚀

From holding a paddle for the first time to winning games in 3.5 open play.

By Forward Pickleball

Imagine starting from scratch — learning how to hold a paddle, choosing the right equipment, and building the fundamentals of the game.

After approximately 5 to 7 hours of training with me, this beginner stepped onto the court recently and started winning games in 3.5-level open play! 💪🎯

📊 After His Very First 2-Hour Round Robin in 3.5 Competitive Play

💬 “I think I played 10 games. Won 5 games, lost 3, and had 2 draws.”

🏆 The coaching approach: Ecological Dynamics

My coaching philosophy focuses on helping players learn through movement, perception, decision-making, and real-game situations — not simply repeating techniques in isolation.

🎯 My message to anyone starting their pickleball journey

  • ✅ Don’t just play recreational games and hope to improve.
  • 🏓 Invest in purposeful drills that develop your skills.
  • 🧠 Learn to read the game, make better decisions, and adapt to different situations.
  • 👨‍🏫 Find a passionate coach who understands how to guide your development, especially in the early stages.
  • 🌱 Build the right foundation from the beginning.

Pickleball is more than hitting a ball over the net. It’s about learning, adapting, and growing — one shot at a time!

Play with purpose. Practice with intention. Grow with passion. 🔥🏓

Results That Speak — Others Were Openly Surprised

After a footwork session and a few top spin and reset baseline coaching sessions, when our student stepped into 3.5 open play and won 7 games out of 11 against players with 5 years of experience — and the other players were stunned. One of them flat-out didn’t believe him when he said he’d only been playing for 2 to 3 months. 😅
