    <h2>Pickleball Ecological Dynamics</h2>
    <p>
      The coaching method behind the programs below &mdash; no memorized
      perfect swing, no lengthy instructions before you hit a ball. Instead,
      the game itself is designed so the right movement emerges: how you
      watch the ball, footwork that finds itself, getting low through your
      hips, and the nose-over-toes check that tells the truth about your
      ready position. Most students feel a clear difference in the first
      few minutes.
    </p>

  <div class="program">
    <h2>Private Lessons</h2>
    <p>
      One-on-one, on-court coaching focused on your specific game. We start
      with a quick assessment of your footwork, paddle control, and shot
      selection, then build a plan around what will move your rating the
      fastest &mdash; whether that's a cleaner dink, better transition-zone
      footwork, or smarter serve returns.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="program">
    <h2>Group Clinics</h2>
    <p>
      Small-group sessions (3&ndash;6 players) built around a single theme:
      third-shot drops, doubles positioning, or defending against a fast
      hands battle. Clinics mix live drilling with point play so you leave
      with reps, not just theory.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="program">
    <h2>Tournament Prep</h2>
    <p>
      Working toward a medal or league match? We'll review your opponents'
      tendencies, tighten your game plan, and run situational drills so
      you're ready for the moments that actually decide matches.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="program">
